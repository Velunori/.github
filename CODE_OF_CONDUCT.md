# Code of Conduct

Velunori welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces managed by the project, including public interactions when representing Velunori.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, clear and patient. Discuss the work and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Give constructive feedback and accept that maintainers and contributors may have different priorities or availability.
- Respect privacy, consent and accessibility needs. Share personal information only with permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate disruption and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Criticism of an implementation is welcome; abuse of the person who wrote it is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

For moderation help, contact repository maintainers using the contact options they publish on their GitHub profiles. If no private contact option is available, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without including the incident's private details. Do not post identifying information or sensitive allegations publicly. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](https://github.com/Velunori/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock a discussion, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity, impact and repetition of the behavior. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid sharing private information beyond what is needed to address the report.
